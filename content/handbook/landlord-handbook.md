**LANDLORD PORTAL GUIDE**

**For Customer Use**

Version 2.3 | August 2026

*Covers: How to manage your properties on the Davors Real Estate
platform*

## Section 1 --- Getting Started

This guide is for landlords using the Davors Real Estate portal. You do
not need a staff account or the main business ERP - your portal is
separate and built specifically for managing your own property
portfolio.

### 1.1 Two Kinds of Landlord Account

There are two kinds of landlord accounts on Davors: Davors-Managed,
where Davors' own team runs the day-to-day property management for you,
and Platform Only, where you manage everything yourself using the
portal. If you're not sure which one you are, check your Dashboard - it
tells you directly under your name.

**Note**

If you're Davors-Managed, most of the portal is view-only for you - you
can see everything happening on your properties, but Davors staff handle
approvals, payouts, and day-to-day actions on your behalf. If you're
Platform Only, you manage everything yourself with no staff approval
needed.

### 1.2 Signing Up

If you don't have an account yet, go to portal.davorsfacilities.com,
choose "I'm a Landlord," and click Sign Up. Fill in your name, email,
phone, and address, and set a password. Your account starts as Platform
Only and Pending - you can log in right away, but you'll see a "Pending
Approval" screen until Davors staff review and approve your account.
Once approved, your real dashboard becomes available.

### 1.3 Already Have an Account?

If Davors staff added your property to the platform on your behalf,
you'll receive an invite by email and SMS once your account is
approved, with a link to set your own password. After that, log in any
time at portal.davorsfacilities.com under "I'm a Landlord."

### 1.4 Getting Your Portfolio Onboarded

Once your account is approved, there are two starting points for getting
your properties into the portal, depending on your situation.

-   **Starting fresh.** if this is the first time your properties have
    been tracked digitally, add your first Property, then its Units,
    Tenants, and Leases directly in the portal as you go --- see
    Section 3. There is no need to have everything ready on day one; add
    what you have and build up from there.

-   **Migrating from another platform.** if you already track your
    portfolio in a spreadsheet or another system, gather the following
    before you begin so you can enter it in one sitting rather than
    several trips back to your old records: for each property, its name
    and address; for each unit, its unit number/name and monthly rent;
    for each tenant, their name, phone, and email; and for each lease,
    the tenant, unit, start date, end date, and rent amount. Entering
    properties before units, and units before leases, avoids having to
    stop partway through to look something up.

**Note**

There is currently no bulk spreadsheet upload for properties or tenants
--- everything is entered through the portal's own forms. Gathering
your information beforehand, in the order above, is the fastest way
through it.

## Section 2 --- Your Dashboard

Your Dashboard is your home screen and gives you an at-a-glance overview
of your whole portfolio:

-   Occupancy - how many of your units are occupied, as a percentage,
    with vacant units called out

-   Rent collected this month, and your outstanding balance

-   Arrears aging - how overdue any unpaid rent is, broken into 0--30,
    31--60, and 60+ day buckets

-   Upcoming lease expirations - leases ending in the next 30/60/90
    days, so renewals don't catch you by surprise

-   A 6-month rent collection trend, and a recent activity feed

-   Open maintenance requests and open complaints

-   **Net Profit (YTD and this month), Total Revenue, and Total Expenses
    ---** a financial summary card with a Month/Year selector so you can
    look back at any period

## Section 3 --- Your Properties, Units, Tenants & Leases

The Real Estate section is where your actual portfolio lives.

-   **Properties & Units ---** Platform Only landlords can add, edit,
    and delete their own properties and units directly (a unit can't be
    deleted if it's occupied or has history). Each unit's status is
    Vacant, Occupied, Under Maintenance, or Application Hold. A Share
    button lets you send a unit's public application link to a
    prospective tenant.

-   **Rental Applications ---** share a unit's application link; when
    someone applies, review their details and Approve or Reject.
    Approving places a temporary hold on the unit while you finalize the
    lease.

-   **Leases ---** Platform Only landlords can create and edit leases
    directly, including setting the rent. Rent changes on an active
    lease need your own confirmation (or, for escalation clauses you've
    already set up, they happen automatically on schedule).

-   **Lease detail page ---** each lease's page is organized into tabs
    --- Overview (lease details and security deposit), Charges (tenant
    charge categories and one-time charges), Documents (signature and
    move-in photos), and More (complaints) --- with a summary bar at the
    top always showing status, rent, and deposit at a glance.

-   **Lease signing ---** every lease has an Acknowledgment panel
    (Unsigned / Sent / Partially Signed / Signed). You can download a
    full tenancy agreement PDF generated from the lease's actual
    details, or upload your own lease document instead if you prefer to
    use your own paperwork - once uploaded, that's what your tenant
    downloads.

-   **Move-in condition photos ---** upload a set of photos documenting
    the unit's condition at move-in, kept separately from the
    property's listing photos. Your tenant sees these (read-only) on
    their own Home screen, alongside the lease's Joint Inspection
    Sheet.

-   **Tenant charge categories ---** on the Charges tab of any lease,
    choose exactly which utilities and service costs are billed to that
    tenant --- Water, Electricity, Refuse, Sewage, Security, Gardening,
    and Service Charge. Every category is off by default; enable only
    the ones you actually want billed. For each one you enable, choose
    Recurring (a flat monthly amount, added automatically each billing
    period by the rent ledger generator) or One-off (nothing generated
    automatically --- you enter the actual charge manually each time a
    bill arrives, useful when the amount varies). Enabling a category
    never affects a lease retroactively --- past charges stay as they
    were, and turning a category off stops future recurring charges
    without deleting anything already billed.

-   **Early termination requests ---** if a tenant asks to end their
    lease early from their own portal, you'll see it here to Approve or
    Reject.

## Section 4 --- Finance

-   **Rent Ledger ---** see every rent period and one-time charge for
    your tenants, and whether it's been paid.

-   **One-Time Charges ---** add an ad-hoc charge to a lease (e.g. a
    repair bill, or a variable utility bill you haven't set up as
    Recurring), optionally tagged with a category (Water, Electricity,
    etc.) so your tenant sees a clear label like "Water" instead of a
    generic charge. Your tenant sees it and pays it alongside their
    rent.

-   **Statements ---** downloadable, printable rent statements for a
    chosen period, useful for your own records or an accountant.

-   **Receipts ---** view, print, or download a receipt for any
    individual rent or one-time-charge payment. Security deposit
    collection and resolution (returned/forfeited/partially forfeited)
    each generate their own downloadable document too.

-   **Expenses ---** log your own running costs against a property
    (repairs, insurance, tax, utilities), with a photo of the receipt
    attached to each entry.

Davors-Managed landlords: your rent is collected into escrow and
remitted to you as a Payout after Davors' management fee is deducted -
you'll see your Payout history here, but Davors staff generate and
remit these on your behalf.

## Section 5 --- Maintenance, Complaints & Terminations

Platform Only landlords approve or reject these themselves.
Davors-Managed landlords see the same information, but Davors staff take
the actions on your behalf.

-   **Maintenance ---** review repair requests from tenants, including
    any where the tenant has offered to fix something themselves at a
    stated cost - approving a self-fix credits that amount against their
    next rent instead of costing you anything out of pocket. When you
    mark a request completed, you can attach "after" photos of the
    finished work - your tenant then sees a before-and-after view of
    their own request.

-   **Complaints ---** respond to a tenant's complaint and mark it
    resolved.

-   **Terminations ---** approve or reject a tenant's request to end
    their lease early.

## Section 6 --- Reports

Vacancy Rate, Occupancy, Arrears Aging, and Income by Property, scoped
to your own portfolio - each exportable to CSV or print for your
records.

## Section 7 --- Announcements

Send a message to your tenants - by email, SMS, and an in-app
notification - targeting all of them, specific properties, specific
leases, or specific tenants by name. Platform Only landlords can compose
and send these themselves.

## Section 8 --- Administration

-   **Billing ---** top up your prepaid SMS credit wallet (used for
    tenant SMS notifications) via card or Mobile Money, and (for
    Platform Only landlords) manage your own per-unit platform billing.

-   **Payment Settings ---** (Platform Only landlords) set up the bank
    account or Mobile Money number your tenants' rent should be paid
    into. Enter your details, confirm the account name Paystack resolves
    against them, and save - your status shows as Active once verified.
    From then on, your tenants' rent payments settle directly to your
    own account automatically.

-   **User Accounts ---** see which of your tenants have activated their
    portal account, resend an invite, reset a tenant's password,
    deactivate or reactivate their portal access, or jump straight to
    their lease.

-   **Workspace Settings ---** your own contact and notification
    details, and your own workspace logo/photo, shown at the top of your
    portal (if you don't upload one, the Davors Facilities logo is
    shown instead).

-   **Account Security ---** change your own password.

## Section 9 --- Quick Reference

  ------------------------------------ ----------------------------------
  **Term**                             **Meaning**

  Davors-Managed                       Davors' own team physically
                                       manages the property day-to-day;
                                       your portal is mostly view-only.

  Platform Only                        You self-manage using the portal
                                       directly, with full control - no
                                       staff approval needed.

  Lease Acknowledgment                 An in-app confirmation that a
                                       lease's terms have been
                                       reviewed - not a legal electronic
                                       signature.

  One-Time Charge                      A single ad-hoc charge added to a
                                       lease (e.g. a repair bill),
                                       separate from recurring rent.

  Escrow                               Where a Davors-Managed landlord's
                                       rent collects before the
                                       management fee is deducted and the
                                       rest is paid out.

  Payout                               The amount actually remitted to a
                                       Davors-Managed landlord after the
                                       management fee is deducted.

  Self-Fix Credit                      An approved cost for a tenant
                                       fixing their own minor repair,
                                       credited against their next rent.
