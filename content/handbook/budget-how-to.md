# Budget — how to set and use it

## What the Budget page is for

The Budget page holds how much you plan to spend each month (or each year) on each type of cost. DavSuite then compares the plan with what was actually spent, so you can see early when a cost is running over.

Find it at **Finance → Budget**.

## Before you start

- **Pick the right business unit.** Budget lines are saved to the business unit selected in the header. Select the unit you are budgeting for (for example your cleaning business) before adding lines.
- **Budget lines use your Expense Categories.** The Category and Subcategory lists come from **Administration → Expense Categories** — the same lists you use in the Expense Register. If a category you need is missing, add it there first.

## Adding a budget line

1. Click **Add budget line**.
2. **Period type** — choose **Monthly** for a normal monthly budget, or **Annual** for a once-a-year amount.
3. **Project / Contract** — choose **Company-wide** to budget for the whole business, or pick one project/contract to budget for that contract only.
4. **Category** and **Subcategory** — pick the expense category, and a subcategory if you want to track something more specific (for example Direct Operational → Chemicals).
5. **Year** and **Month** — the period the budget is for.
6. **Budget amount** — the planned spend in GHS.
7. **Notes** (optional) — a short reminder of what the line covers.
8. Save.

### Tips

- **Use one level per category.** Do not add a whole-category line *and* subcategory lines for the same category in the same month — spending would be counted against both. Either budget the whole category once, or split it into subcategories.
- **Company-wide is simplest for staff costs.** When staff work across several projects, budget Staff Salaries company-wide.
- Monthly and annual budgets can exist side by side for the same category.

## Copying a budget to a new month

You do not need to retype the budget every month.

- **When you open a month that has no budget lines**, the table shows "No budget lines for {month}" with three options: **Copy from {previous month}**, **Choose another month**, or **Add budget line**.
- At any time you can click **Copy budget…** (next to Add budget line).

In the Copy budget window:

1. **From** — the month to copy from (defaults to the month before).
2. **To** — the month to copy into (defaults to the month you are viewing).
3. The list shows every monthly line in the From month. All new lines are ticked; untick any you don't want.
4. Lines that already exist in the To month are greyed out and marked **"Already in {month}"** — they are never copied twice.
5. The footer shows the total to copy and how many lines are skipped.
6. Click **Copy N lines**. DavSuite switches to the To month so you can review and edit amounts.

Only monthly lines are copied. Annual lines are not copied.

## Checking budget against actual spend

- Click **View budget vs actual** on the Budget page, or open **Reports → Budget vs Actual**.
- Each line shows Budget, Actual, Variance, Variance %, Remaining and a status (for example **Over budget** on the report; on the Director's Scorecard the same bands show as **On track**, **Near limit** or **Over budget**).
- Actual spending comes from the Expense Register (and purchases for inventory categories). For company-wide Staff Salaries, actuals come from posted payroll expenses; for a single project, they come from payroll matched to that project.
- Directors also see the budget comparison on the Dashboard in the **Director's Scorecard**, where each line links to the matching expenses.

## A simple monthly routine

1. **Start of month:** open the new month and use **Copy from {previous month}**. Adjust any amounts that will change.
2. **During the month:** record expenses with the correct category, subcategory and project so they match the budget.
3. **Mid-month and month end:** check Budget vs Actual. Anything **Over budget** — find out why before the next month.
