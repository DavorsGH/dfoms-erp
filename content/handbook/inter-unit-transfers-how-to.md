# Inter-unit Transfers (staff)

## What they are

If your workspace has more than one business unit, **Inter-unit Transfers** record money moving **between your own business units** — for example when one unit pays a bill that belongs to another. They are **not income or expenses** and do not change profit. They only track which unit owes which.

Example: your cleaning business pays GHS 500 for the software business's subscription. Record the expense in the software business, and record a transfer of GHS 500 **from** the cleaning business **to** the software business. The software business now owes the cleaning business GHS 500.

## Record a transfer

1. Open **Finance → Inter-unit Transfers** (shown when you have at least two business units).
2. Click **Add transfer**.
3. Enter **Date**, **From** business unit, **To** business unit, **Amount**, **Description** and, if you have one, a **Reference**.
4. Save.

## Read the summary

The **Summary** card shows the net position between each pair of units, for example **"Unit A owes Unit B GHS …"**, based on all transfers up to the date shown.

## Effect on cash and the Balance Sheet

- Viewing **one business unit**: the transfer moves cash out of the "From" unit and into the "To" unit, and the Balance Sheet shows **Due from other business units** (money owed to this unit) or **Due to other business units** (money this unit owes).
- Viewing **All businesses**: transfers between your own units cancel out, so they do not appear.

## Ask the assistant

Ask "What does [unit] owe [unit]?" — the assistant uses the same transfers and summary as this page.
