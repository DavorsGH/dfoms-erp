**FACILITY MANAGER PORTAL GUIDE**

*Covers: How Facility Managers use the Davors portal for assigned
properties*

## Section 1 --- What a Facility Manager Is

A Facility Manager is a caretaker or property manager a landlord invites
to handle day-to-day work on their properties, with their own separate
login --- scoped to only the properties the landlord assigns, and only
the tasks they allow.

This role applies on Platform Only portfolios. If a property is
Davors-Managed, Davors' own team already fills this role.

If a landlord has invited you as their Facility Manager, this guide is
written directly for you. The landlord portal guide describes the full
landlord side; this handbook covers your own role, capabilities, and
portal.

## Section 2 --- Accepting Your Invite & Logging In

### 2.1 Accepting your invite

You'll receive an email with a link to set your own password. This
creates your own separate login --- never shared with the landlord's
account or any other role you might hold on the platform.

### 2.2 Logging in

Go to portal.davorsfacilities.com and choose "I'm a Facility Manager."
You'll only see the properties the landlord has assigned you to.

## Section 3 --- What You Can Do

Your dashboard only shows the tabs the landlord has switched on for you:

-   **Maintenance** --- log and update repair requests

-   **Complaints** --- file one, or respond to one raised against a
    tenant

-   **Inspections**

-   **Services** --- log a cleaning or gardening visit, with its cost

-   **Collections** --- if you're allowed to collect rent or charges

## Section 4 --- Collecting Rent or Charges

If collecting rent or charges is switched on for you, you log the cash
or Mobile Money amount you received and who from. It shows as pending
until the landlord confirms it on their end --- it is not marked as paid
until they do, so always let the tenant know their payment is confirmed
once you see it move to Confirmed, not before.

**Note**

Nothing hits the landlord's Rent Ledger as paid until they confirm or
reject the collection, so a collection can never be marked paid without
the landlord's say-so.

## Section 5 --- What You Can't See

The landlord's financials, payouts, bank or Mobile Money account
details, leases, or account settings are never visible to you, whatever
your assigned capabilities.

If you also work with more than one landlord on Davors, each needs to
invite you separately, and you'll need a distinct email for each if you
already hold another role on the platform (staff, tenant, or another
landlord's Facility Manager).
